## 1. Purpose
Evaluate the pre-registered kill-triggers against the audited MVP results to decide
whether the CP2 hypothesis is viable, weak, or refuted.

## 2. Pre-start audit
Inspect CP2-T046 audit; `docs/DATP_CP_Roadmap.md` §12 (kill-triggers / go-no-go).

## 3. Scope
Evaluate each pre-registered kill-trigger, e.g.:
- HIGH/LOW produce no significant directional Δτ on eligible feasible victims.
- B1 vs B2 vs B4 show **no** policy-differentiated blast radius.
- RANDOM control is **not** near-null (confound).
- AUROC moves materially (would break the calibration-only framing).
- feasibility coverage too low for meaningful claims.
Record the outcome per trigger with evidence; do not fabricate significance.
