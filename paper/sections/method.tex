\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Dataset and Federation}

The evaluation uses N-BaIoT~\cite{meidan2018nbaiot}, a labeled IoT traffic dataset covering nine physical devices across five types (doorbell, baby monitor, security camera, thermostat, webcam), each device forming one federation client; all nine satisfy the $\ge$100-sample calibration eligibility threshold (size range in Table~\ref{tab:repro}).
Each device's benign stream is partitioned chronologically into a $60\%$ train / $1\%$ gap / $20\%$ calibration / $1\%$ gap / $18\%$ test split, the gaps avoiding temporal leakage, and training and calibration use benign traffic only.
Attack-labeled records are scaled with each device's training-fitted scaler and used only as the positive class for TPR; they never enter training, calibration, poisoning reservoirs, or injected buffers.
The federation trains a shared autoencoder on benign traffic using FedAvg~\cite{mcmahan2017fedavg} (Table~\ref{tab:repro}); each client then scores its own traffic with the aggregated model and enters the threshold-calibration stage at $q{=}0.95$.

\subsection{Threshold Policies}

Three threshold-policy families are evaluated, covering the design points surveyed in Section~\ref{sec:background}.

\begin{description}
  \item[\GLOBAL.]
  \[
    \tau_i^{\mathrm{local}} = \operatorname{quantile}(\mathcal{C}_i,q),
    \qquad
    \tau^{\mathrm{global}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\tau_i^{\mathrm{local}} .
  \]
  Every client receives the same $\tau^{\mathrm{global}}$, so a single poisoned local threshold contributes only $1/N$ of the shared shift.

  \item[\LOCAL.] Each client retains $\tau_i = \tau_i^{\mathrm{local}}$.
  Shifts are confined to the compromised client; no cross-client threshold propagation.

  \item[\CLUSTER.]
  Clients are grouped on a standard-scaled 4-feature fingerprint (mean, std, skew, $p_{95}$ of $\mathcal{C}_i$) via $k$-means++~\cite{arthur2007kmeanspp} ($K{=}3$, random seed 42; clustering recomputed after each attack draw so a poisoned fingerprint can change the compromised client's cluster assignment).
  The shared threshold is the cluster mean, so a compromised client may perturb the threshold for co-cluster members (\emph{intra-cluster threshold perturbation}) while other clusters are unaffected.
  Under Threshold-Raise, tail injection raises the mean, $p_{95}$, and dispersion components of the fingerprint, displacing the victim, in the runs observed here, into an effectively singleton cluster whose mean collapses to the victim's poisoned local threshold; this yields near-\LOCAL victim shifts while co-cluster members see perturbations small relative to the victim shift but not identically zero.
\end{description}

\subsection{Attack Parameterization}

For each (policy, objective, injection fraction $f \in \{0.0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4\}$) combination the protocol runs 10 seeds, each fixing a training configuration and an independent poisoning draw; a clean copy of the calibration array is preserved and only the poisoned copy is used for threshold computation.
The four source-objective pairs are High-Score-Benign + Threshold-Raise (primary), Low-Score-Benign + Threshold-Lower, and Random-Benign with each objective as matched negative controls.
The attack is applied to each of the nine clients as victim in turn (single-client scope), yielding 4~source-objective pairs $\times$ 3~threshold policies $\times$ 4~injection fractions $\times$ 9~victim clients $\times$ 10~seeds $= 4{,}320$ evaluated cells, 3,240 of them with non-zero injection; the 1,080 cells at $f{=}0$ are the clean comparators and coincide across source-objective pairs.
Training seed $i$ is combined with poisoning seed $i{+}100$ and analysis seed $i{+}300$, separating training, calibration-sampling, and resampling stochasticity.

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}

Clean and poisoned conditions are paired: for a policy, victim $v$, and seed $s$, the clean comparator is the $f{=}0$ cell produced by the same trained model and the same calibration array before replacement, so every reported difference is within-seed and within-victim,
\[
  \Delta\tau_{v,s} \;=\; \tau^{\mathrm{poisoned}}_{v,s} - \tau^{\mathrm{clean}}_{v,s},
\]
and likewise for $\Delta\mathrm{TPR}$ and $\Delta\mathrm{CV(FPR)}$.
The nine victim values within a seed are averaged into one seed-level aggregate, and the ten paired aggregates are the inferential units: bootstrap resampling is over those ten seeds, never over the ninety victim--seed cells, which share a trained model within a seed and are descriptive support, not independent runs.
Three gates, all fixed before any poisoned run, structure the claims below.

\textbf{Gate 1 (material shift, primary gate)} evaluates the signed displacement $\Delta\tau_{v,s}$ at the victim--seed cell.
A cell is \emph{material} when $|\Delta\tau_{v,s}| > \delta\tau_{v,s}$ and its sign matches the objective---positive for raising, negative for lowering---so displacement in the wrong direction never counts, where
\[
  \delta\tau_{v,s} = \max\!\bigl(0.1\,\mathrm{IQR}(\mathcal{C}_{v,s}^{\mathrm{clean}}),\;
  0.01\,\mathrm{median}_j(\mathrm{IQR}(\mathcal{C}_{j,s}^{\mathrm{clean}}))\bigr)
\]
is computed from the same seed's clean calibration arrays with $j$ over all nine clients.
A \emph{seed passes} when at least 5 of the 9 clients, each taken as victim in turn, are material, and a (policy, fraction) configuration \emph{passes Gate 1} when at least 8 of the 10 seeds pass; these majorities are conservative consistency requirements, not calibrated error rates.
Passing supports the claim that the channel displaces thresholds by more than clean-baseline instability at a majority of victims and reproducibly across seeds; failure would mean the displacement is not separable from that instability.
Both coefficients were locked from clean-artifact statistics beforehand, the 10\% IQR term exceeding the threshold instability observed across clean seeds and the 1\% cross-client floor stopping clients with near-zero score spread from counting as material on arbitrarily small shifts.
Gate 1 is thus a pre-committed materiality floor rather than a significance test; its pass rates are reported alongside the effect sizes in Table~\ref{tab:main-results}.

\textbf{Gate 2 (directional excess over control)} is an objective-matched paired contrast: within each cell the control $\Delta\tau$ is subtracted from the attack $\Delta\tau$, sign-oriented by objective, and the nine victim values are averaged to one value per seed.
A configuration passes when at least 8 of the 10 seed values are strictly positive and their median is positive, with a percentile bootstrap CI on those ten contrast values computed alongside but not entering the rule.

\textbf{Gate 3 (downstream operating-point displacement)} evaluates the mean victim $\Delta\mathrm{TPR}$ at the seed level and passes when its direction matches the threshold objective---negative for raising, positive for lowering---in at least 8 of the 10 seeds; failure means the operating point did not move consistently with the threshold shift.
For raising, the threshold increase means reduced victim TPR and hence missed-detection harm.
For lowering, the threshold decrease means a higher false-positive rate and, depending on policy, greater cross-client FPR disparity, and the accompanying positive $\Delta\mathrm{TPR}$ is evidence of operating-point displacement only; the harm metric for that objective is $\Delta\mathrm{CV(FPR)}$.

All CIs are 95\% percentile bootstrap over the 10 seed-level aggregates with $B{=}10{,}000$ resamples, reported per configuration, two-sided and without multiplicity adjustment; at ten paired seeds they are approximate uncertainty estimates, not exact coverage guarantees.

\subsection{Isolation Sanity Check}

To confirm that the attack is confined to the calibration stage, the protocol verifies that (i)~AUROC is invariant under poisoning, (ii)~per-client rank ordering of clean anomaly scores is unchanged, and (iii)~model weights before and after calibration are bitwise identical; all three hold in every cell, including the 3,240 with non-zero injection.
Being invariant by construction, these quantities verify implementation isolation rather than provide independent evidence.
